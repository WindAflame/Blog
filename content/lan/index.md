Video games have evolved, and more and more titles require a constant Internet connection, limiting the possibility of offline games with friends.

## What is a LAN game?

A LAN game is a multiplayer game played over a local network, without the need for an Internet connection.

Unfortunately, today's games have DRM and require connection to online Internet servers. For example, Worms W.M.D. requires an Internet connection because of its DRM, as does Call of Duty, which requires an online server.
